# Mastercard — Cybersecurity Virtual Experience Program

**Role simulated:** Security Analyst, Security Awareness team

Mastercard's simulation focuses on phishing — one of the most common threats organizations face — and demonstrates that effective security work doesn't always require a deep technical background, just an understanding of how attackers exploit human behavior.

### Task 1 — Design a Phishing Email Simulation

- Took an obviously fake phishing email and reworked it to be more convincing
- Learned what different types of phishing emails look like and the threat phishing poses to an organization
- Explored how Mastercard prevents and mitigates phishing attacks at scale

### Task 2 — Interpret Phishing Simulation Results

- Analyzed the performance data from the phishing simulation to identify which teams were most susceptible
- Built a short presentation recommending targeted security awareness training for the affected teams
- Practiced translating raw simulation results into actionable, business-facing recommendations

### Skills demonstrated
`social engineering awareness` · `phishing analysis` · `security awareness training design` · `stakeholder presentation`
