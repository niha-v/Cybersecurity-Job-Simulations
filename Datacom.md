# Datacom Cybersecurity Simulation

**Role simulated:** Cybersecurity team member

Datacom's simulation is designed for beginners and covers the fast-paced world of cybersecurity operations across two of the field's core "streams": Governance, Risk & Compliance and Threat Intelligence & Analysis.
 
### Task 1 — Security Breach Impact Analysis: Orion Health Services
 
Acted as a Cybersecurity Analyst Graduate investigating a live ransomware incident at Orion Health Services, a mid-sized healthcare technology provider (250 employees) serving clinics across Australia and New Zealand.
 
**Scenario:** Unusual outbound traffic was spotted on an internal server Monday morning; by midday, employees were locked out of systems and a ransom note appeared on the shared drive.
 
- **Entry point:** phishing email to a finance team member with a malicious Excel attachment
- **Compromised data:** employee payroll records, patient appointment schedules, internal system credentials
- **Systems affected:** file server, HR/finance systems, partially-encrypted backup server
- **Indicators of compromise:** suspicious login from an overseas IP, Mimikatz used for credential harvesting, files encrypted with a `.orionlock` extension
Produced a **Security Breach Impact Report** for the executive team covering the attack timeline, exploited weaknesses, and business/legal/reputational consequences of a ransomware attack on a healthcare provider handling patient data — plus recommendations to close the gaps.
 
### Task 2 — Cybersecurity Risk Assessment: RetailNova Pty Ltd
 
Reviewed RetailNova, a Melbourne-based retail company (~1,200 staff, $450M annual revenue) selling consumer electronics and lifestyle products through 85 physical stores and an e-commerce platform.
 
**Infrastructure reviewed:** custom e-commerce site/app integrated with PayPal and Afterpay, cloud-connected POS systems, Salesforce (CRM), SAP (ERP — inventory, finance, HR), AWS hosting, RBAC-based access control, BYOD policy with VPN remote access, and Teams/Slack for internal comms.
 
**Data at risk:** customer PII (names, emails, phone numbers, addresses), tokenized payment details, purchase history, behavioral analytics, and loyalty program data.
 
**Prior incident history factored into the assessment:** a 2023 phishing attack that compromised customer service rep credentials, a 2024 ransomware attempt stopped by endpoint protection (still caused 2 hours of downtime), and a 2025 third-party marketing vendor data leak exposing 5,000 customer emails.
 
Using a Threat and Vulnerability Identification template, I mapped RetailNova's key assets against plausible threats, assessed likelihood and impact, and compiled a **Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Report** prioritizing the highest-risk gaps and recommending mitigations.
 
### Skills demonstrated
`risk assessment` · `threat analysis` · `breach impact analysis` · `ransomware incident investigation` · `governance, risk & compliance (GRC)` · `executive-facing security reporting`
