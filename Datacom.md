# Datacom — Cybersecurity Simulation

**Role simulated:** Cybersecurity team member

Datacom's simulation is designed for beginners and covers the fast-paced world of cybersecurity operations across two of the field's core "streams": Governance, Risk & Compliance and Threat Intelligence & Analysis.

### Task 1 — Security Breach Impact Analysis

Acted as a cybersecurity consultant investigating what went wrong after a cyber attack.

- Analyzed the damage caused by the attack and identified the weaknesses that were exploited
- Assessed how the attack affected the organization's business, legal standing, and reputation
- Delivered clear, business-focused recommendations — core skills in threat analysis and incident management

### Task 2 — Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

Practiced systematically identifying and prioritizing organizational risk.

- Identified the company's most valuable technology and data assets
- Mapped the threats that put those assets at risk and assessed their likelihood
- Prioritized risks and compiled findings into a report to help the organization address issues before they become real incidents

### Skills demonstrated
`risk assessment` · `threat analysis` · `breach impact analysis` · `governance, risk & compliance (GRC)` · `security reporting`
